The Value

Predictable Oracle Operations Without the Overhead

Maintaining an in-house Oracle team with the depth to cover Fusion Cloud, quarterly updates, integrations, and compliance is expensive and difficult. NIRA LLC's managed services model gives you expert Oracle coverage at a predictable cost — with SLA commitments and direct access to senior consultants.

  • SLA-backed response times with defined escalation paths
  • Proactive quarterly update management — no surprises when Oracle pushes updates
  • Continuous improvement recommendations based on Oracle best practices
  • Single point of contact for all Oracle application matters
  • Flexible scope — expand or reduce coverage as your business evolves
  • Transparent monthly reporting on tickets, changes, and system health

💡 Right-Sized for Your Organization

We design managed services engagements around your actual Oracle footprint and business complexity — not a one-size-fits-all package. Monthly retainer or consumption-based models available.

🔄 Oracle Quarterly Updates

Oracle Fusion Cloud updates quarterly. We read the release notes, identify impact on your configuration, test affected processes in your test environment, and prepare your users — every quarter, without fail.

What We Manage

⚙️

Oracle Application Administration

Day-to-day Oracle system administration — user provisioning, role management, approval workflow maintenance, system configuration updates, and scheduled process monitoring across your Oracle Fusion Cloud modules.

🔄

Quarterly Update Management

End-to-end management of Oracle's quarterly cloud updates — release note analysis, test environment validation, regression testing of critical business processes, and user communication before production cutover.

Finance BPO

Business process outsourcing for Oracle Financials operations — AP invoice processing, AR cash application, GL journal entry review, bank reconciliation, and month-end close support delivered by our Oracle-certified finance team.

HR & Payroll BPO

Oracle HCM operational support — employee data maintenance, payroll processing oversight, absence management, benefits administration, and HR reporting — allowing your HR team to focus on people strategy rather than system operations.

Performance & Health Reporting

Monthly Oracle environment health reports — covering system performance, integration success rates, open issues, change requests completed, and upcoming Oracle release impacts — keeping your leadership informed.
