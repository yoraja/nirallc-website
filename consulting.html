Our Approach

True Techno-Functional Consulting

Unlike purely functional or purely technical consultants, NIRA LLC operates at the intersection of both — understanding your business processes deeply while having the technical capability to configure, extend, and integrate Oracle systems to match them precisely.

This means fewer handoffs, faster delivery, and solutions that actually work the way your business operates.

  • Business process analysis and requirements mapping before any configuration begins
  • Full lifecycle delivery — design, build, test, train, go-live, and post-go-live support
  • On-site presence for critical project phases; remote delivery for development and support
  • Senior oversight on every engagement with internal quality reviews before delivery
  • Detailed project repositories maintained throughout for long-term supportability

💼 Engagement Models

We work with clients on fixed-scope implementations, time-and-materials engagements, and hybrid models — adapting to your procurement and governance requirements.

🌍 Global Delivery

On-site consulting worldwide combined with a remote delivery center for cost-effective development, testing, and support activities across time zones.

🔒 Quality Guarantee

If subcontracted work does not meet our standards, we correct it at our expense — never passed on to the client. Our internal review process ensures every deliverable is validated before handover.

Service Areas

What We Implement & Support

Our Oracle consulting spans the full application portfolio — cloud-first and on-premise.

☁️

Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP

End-to-end Fusion Cloud implementations — ledger setup, subledger configuration, workflow design, reporting, and integration with third-party systems. We follow Oracle's prescribed methodology while adapting to your organization's specific needs.

💰

Oracle Financials Cloud

General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Fixed Assets, Cash Management, and Expenses. We configure automated reconciliations, approval workflows, bank integrations, and period-close processes for efficient financial operations.

🏢

Oracle E-Business Suite (EBS)

Full EBS implementations and upgrades — Financials, HRMS, SCM, and custom module development. We handle personalizations, custom reports (BI Publisher, Oracle Reports), interfaces, conversions, and extensions using Oracle's AIM methodology.

🔗

Oracle Fusion Middleware

Integration architecture using SOA Suite, Oracle Service Bus, ADF, and WebLogic. We design and build system-to-system integrations, REST/SOAP APIs, and middleware platforms that connect Oracle with your broader enterprise landscape.

📦

Oracle SCM & Procurement

Order Management, Inventory, Purchasing, Sourcing, and Supplier Management on Oracle Fusion Cloud. We configure supply chain flows, approval hierarchies, supplier portals, and procurement analytics.

👥

Oracle HCM Cloud

Core HR, Payroll, Talent Management, and Workforce Management implementations. We map HR processes to Oracle HCM's data model, configure fast formulas, set up payroll elements, and build self-service journeys for employees and managers.

Methodology

How We Deliver Projects

  • 1

    Discovery & Assessment

    We review your current Oracle environment, business processes, pain points, and goals. This shapes the project scope and identifies risks early.

  • 2

    Solution Design

    Functional design documents (FDDs) and technical design documents (TDDs) are produced, reviewed with your team, and signed off before any build begins.

  • 3

    Build & Configuration

    Configuration, development of custom components, data conversion scripts, and integrations are built in a development environment with internal QA reviews.

  • 4

    Testing (SIT & UAT)

    System Integration Testing followed by User Acceptance Testing with your team. We create test scripts, support UAT execution, and track defects to resolution.

  • 5

    Training & Go-Live

    End-user training, cutover planning, data migration execution, and hypercare support immediately post go-live to ensure a smooth transition.

  • 6

    Post Go-Live Support

    Structured hypercare followed by transition to support — with detailed documentation handed over and repository access maintained for future reference.
