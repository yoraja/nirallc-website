Full Oracle Financials Cloud Suite

Oracle Financials Cloud is the foundation of Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP — providing a modern, integrated financial management platform with AI-powered automation, real-time analytics, and built-in compliance controls. NIRA LLC's team holds Oracle Financials Cloud certifications and has deep hands-on experience across all core modules.

  • Certified in Oracle Financials Cloud Payables Implementation (2020)
  • Full chart of accounts design, ledger setup, and accounting rule configuration
  • Automated reconciliations, period-close checklists, and intercompany elimination
  • Bank integration, electronic payments, and cash forecasting
  • Fixed asset lifecycle management and depreciation automation
  • Expense report automation with policy enforcement and approval workflows

Modules

Financials Cloud Modules

📒

General Ledger

Chart of accounts design, ledger configuration, journal entry automation, allocations, multi-currency revaluation, intercompany accounting, and Financial Reporting Studio for management and statutory reporting.

📥

Accounts Payable

Invoice processing with intelligent account defaulting, three-way matching, payment terms, supplier payment processing (checks, EFT, wire), early payment discount optimization, and 1099 reporting.

📤

Accounts Receivable

Invoice generation, cash application, collections management, revenue recognition, credit management, aging reports, and predictive collections analytics to improve cash flow.

🏗️

Fixed Assets

Asset books (corporate and tax), depreciation methods, mass additions from AP and Projects, asset retirement, revaluation, and audit-ready asset register reports for compliance and financial reporting.

🏦

Cash Management

Bank account setup, bank statement import and auto-reconciliation, cash positioning, cash forecasting, and in-house banking for treasury operations.

🧾

Expenses

Mobile expense capture, mileage calculation, per diem policies, corporate card reconciliation, multi-level approval workflows, and expense analytics for spend visibility and policy compliance.
