What is Noetix?

Pre-Built Oracle EBS Reporting, Ready to Use

Noetix (now part of the Angles for Oracle suite by insightsoftware) is the leading real-time operational reporting and analytics solution purpose-built for Oracle E-Business Suite. It delivers more out-of-the-box Oracle EBS content than any other reporting vendor — eliminating the months of effort typically required to build a custom reporting layer on top of Oracle's complex data model.

NIRA LLC implements and supports Noetix for Oracle EBS customers who need immediate access to accurate operational reports without custom development — covering Financials, HRMS, SCM, and cross-process analytics.

  • 1,800+ pre-built reports covering all major Oracle EBS modules out of the box
  • Real-time access to Oracle EBS operational data without a separate data warehouse
  • Context-aware data model that understands Oracle EBS business processes, not just tables
  • Cross-process reporting — combine Financials, Procurement, HR, and SCM data in a single report
  • Native integration with Power BI, Tableau, Snowflake, and Azure for modern analytics
  • NoetixViews generates database views that expose Oracle EBS data in business-friendly terms

Capabilities

Noetix Reporting Capabilities

📋

NoetixViews

Pre-built database views that expose Oracle EBS data in business-friendly, human-readable terms — eliminating the need for developers to understand Oracle's complex table structure. Works with any SQL-based reporting tool.

💰

Financials Reporting

Pre-built reports for General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Fixed Assets, and Cash Management — covering trial balances, aging reports, payment analysis, asset registers, and period-close analytics.

👥

HRMS Reporting

HR and payroll reports including headcount analysis, turnover metrics, compensation reporting, absence analytics, and workforce planning data — all sourced directly from Oracle HRMS in real time.

📦

Supply Chain & Procurement Analytics

Purchasing spend analysis, supplier performance metrics, inventory valuation, order fulfillment rates, and supply chain KPIs — cross-process reporting connecting procurement, inventory, and order management data.

📈

Noetix Analytics

Packaged analytics for Oracle EBS with pre-built data warehouse architecture, ETL, data models, and dashboards — reducing the effort of a full data warehouse strategy while delivering executive-level analytics and KPI tracking.

🔗

Power BI & Tableau Integration

Noetix Generator for Power BI and Tableau simplifies access to Oracle EBS data for both operational and strategic reporting — allowing business users to build self-service analytics on Oracle data using familiar tools.
