Oracle E-Business Suite remains a mission-critical platform for thousands of organizations worldwide. Whether you need ongoing EBS support, a specific customization, an upgrade, or are planning your migration path to Oracle Fusion Cloud, our team has the depth to help.

  • Full EBS Financials suite — GL, AP, AR, FA, Cash Management, and Purchasing
  • EBS HRMS — HR, Payroll, iRecruitment, and Self-Service HR
  • EBS SCM — Order Management, Inventory, WIP, and BOM
  • Technical development — Oracle Reports, BI Publisher, SQL/PLSQL, OAF, and Workflow
  • RICE object development — interfaces, conversions, extensions, and custom reports
  • EBS to Oracle Fusion Cloud migration planning and execution

Our EBS technical consultants are proficient in Oracle Forms, Oracle Reports, BI Publisher, PL/SQL, OAF (Oracle Application Framework), Oracle Workflow, and Oracle XML Gateway — covering the full EBS technical stack.
