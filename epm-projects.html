Oracle EPM Cloud & Project Management

Enterprise Performance Management and Project Management on Oracle Cloud — connecting financial planning, consolidation, and project execution in a unified platform.

Discuss Your EPM Project
Overview

Drive Financial Performance with Oracle EPM & Projects

Oracle EPM Cloud provides a suite of cloud-based performance management tools — planning, budgeting, forecasting, financial consolidation, and tax reporting — that integrate with Oracle Financials Cloud for a complete financial management ecosystem. Combined with Oracle Project Management Cloud, it enables organizations to plan, execute, and report on projects with full financial visibility.

  • Rolling forecasts and driver-based planning models replacing static annual budgets
  • Financial consolidation and close automation across complex multi-entity structures
  • Tax provision and reporting automation for compliance efficiency
  • Project costing and billing integrated with Financials Cloud GL and AR
  • Resource management and project analytics for service-based organizations
  • Profitability and cost management for granular margin analysis

📊 Planning & Budgeting

Oracle Planning and Budgeting Cloud replaces spreadsheet-based budgeting with a centralized, workflow-driven process — reducing cycle time and improving forecast accuracy.

🔗 Financials Integration

Oracle EPM Cloud integrates bidirectionally with Oracle Financials Cloud — actuals flow in automatically, approved budgets feed back to GL, creating a closed-loop planning and reporting cycle.

Modules

EPM & Project Modules We Implement

📈

Oracle Planning & Budgeting Cloud

Driver-based planning models, workforce planning, capital planning, rolling forecasts, and what-if scenario analysis. Replaces disconnected spreadsheet processes with a governed, auditable planning cycle.

🏦

Financial Consolidation & Close

Legal and management consolidation, intercompany elimination, currency translation, minority interest calculations, and close task management for multi-entity organizations. Reduces consolidation cycle time dramatically.

🧾

Oracle Tax Reporting Cloud

Country-by-Country (CbCR) reporting, deferred tax calculations, tax provision automation, and BEPS compliance reporting — integrated with your consolidation process for efficient tax close.

💹

Profitability & Cost Management

Cost allocation models, profitability analysis by product, customer, channel, or region, and activity-based costing — providing the margin insights needed for strategic decision making.

📋

Oracle Project Management Cloud

Project planning, resource management, task assignment, project costing, and milestone tracking. Integrates with Financials Cloud for project billing, revenue recognition, and cost capitalization.

💰

Oracle Project Costing & Billing

Labor and expense costing, overhead allocation, contract management, milestone billing, and project revenue recognition — supporting both fixed-price and time-and-materials contracts.
