Our Philosophy

Training That Actually Sticks

Generic Oracle training teaches the product. NIRA LLC teaches your team how to use Oracle in the context of your specific business processes, your configuration, and your data.

We deliver training after implementation — when users have a real system to learn on — and structure it by role so every participant only learns what is relevant to their job.

  • Role-based curriculum — AP clerks, GL accountants, procurement managers, and IT admins each get targeted training
  • Hands-on exercises in your Oracle environment using your actual data and processes
  • Delivered on-site or virtually — adapting to your team's location and schedule
  • Training materials and quick reference guides provided for ongoing reference
  • Post-training Q&A sessions and follow-up support included

🎯 Who We Train

End users, power users, system administrators, IT support teams, and finance/HR/procurement managers — anyone who needs to operate, maintain, or support your Oracle environment.

📅 Training Formats

Instructor-led classroom sessions, virtual instructor-led training (VILT), one-on-one power user coaching, and self-service reference guide packages — whichever format works for your team.

Training Areas

What We Train On

💰

Oracle Financials Training

End-user and administrator training on General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Fixed Assets, Cash Management, and Expenses — covering transaction processing, period close, reporting, and reconciliation workflows.

🛒

Procurement & SCM Training

Training for purchasing teams, procurement managers, and inventory controllers on Oracle Procurement Cloud and SCM — covering requisitions, POs, supplier management, receiving, and supply chain planning.

👥

Oracle HCM Training

HR administrator and manager training on Core HR, Payroll, and Talent — covering worker lifecycle transactions, payroll processing, absence management, and performance management in Oracle HCM Cloud.

🌿

Oracle Redwood Training

Dedicated Redwood adoption training for teams migrating from Classic UI — covering new navigation patterns, personalization tools, guided journeys, and AI-assisted features in the Redwood experience.

🛡️

Oracle Security & Administration

Technical training for Oracle system administrators on user provisioning, role management, security policies, audit configuration, and Oracle Identity Governance — including SOX-relevant security controls.

📊

Oracle Reporting & Analytics

Training on Oracle OTBI (Oracle Transactional Business Intelligence), Financial Reporting Studio, Smart View, and FBDI data loading — empowering your team to build and run their own reports without IT dependency.
