What Is Redwood?

Oracle Redwood — More Than a UI Refresh

Oracle Redwood is Oracle's next-generation design system and experience framework for all Fusion Cloud applications. It replaces the Classic UI with a clean, modern, AI-integrated interface that works consistently across HCM, ERP, SCM, and CX modules.

Redwood is not just cosmetic — it introduces new interaction patterns, embedded AI agents, guided journeys, and role-based work areas that change how users complete tasks.

  • Unified UI across all Fusion Cloud modules — consistent experience for all users
  • Slide-out panels and contextual actions replace dense toolbars and pop-ups
  • Embedded AI assistance and smart suggestions reduce manual steps
  • Guided journeys allow step-by-step embedded instructions with links, videos, and dashboards
  • Mobile-responsive design for on-the-go access and approvals
  • Role-based work areas with analytics tiles and quick-action shortcuts

📊 Business Benefits

Organizations that have migrated to Redwood report reduced training time, higher user satisfaction scores, and faster task completion — particularly for approvals, expense entry, and period-close workflows.

🤖 AI Integration

Redwood surfaces Oracle's embedded AI capabilities — including Oracle Fusion AI Agent Studio workflows — directly within the user interface, enabling intelligent automation at the point of work.

📱 Mobile-First

Redwood passes the mobile test — all pages are responsive and usable on phones and tablets without separate mobile apps, enabling true workforce mobility.

Our Approach

How NIRA LLC Manages Your Redwood Migration

  • 1

    Impact Assessment

    We inventory all Classic UI pages your users currently access, identify customizations (personalizations, flexfields, HCM journeys), and map each to its Redwood equivalent — flagging gaps requiring remediation.

  • 2

    Module-by-Module Migration Plan

    We prioritize modules by business impact and readiness — typically starting with Financials and Procurement before moving to HCM and SCM — building a phased timeline that avoids peak business periods.

  • 3

    Personalization Re-implementation

    Existing Classic UI personalizations (saved searches, custom columns, page layouts) are rebuilt in Redwood's new personalization framework — ensuring users retain their tailored workspaces.

  • 4

    Guided Journey Setup

    We design and configure Oracle Guided Journeys for key business processes — embedding instructions, approval checklists, and links to relevant reports directly in the Redwood interface to support user adoption.

  • 5

    User Acceptance Testing

    Role-based UAT with your key users validates that all business processes work correctly in Redwood before go-live — with defect tracking and rapid resolution.

  • 6

    Training & Adoption

    Role-specific training sessions, quick reference guides, and embedded guided journeys ensure users are confident in Redwood from day one — minimizing productivity dip during cutover.
