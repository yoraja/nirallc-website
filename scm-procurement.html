Overview

Connected Supply Chain & Procurement on Oracle Cloud

Oracle SCM Cloud and Procurement Cloud provide an integrated platform that connects your suppliers, procurement operations, inventory, and order fulfillment in real time. NIRA LLC implements these modules with a focus on practical business outcomes — reduced procurement cycle times, improved inventory turns, and better supplier relationships.

  • Procure-to-pay process fully automated — from requisition through supplier payment
  • Supplier portal for invoice submission, PO acknowledgment, and communication
  • Real-time inventory visibility across warehouses and locations
  • Demand-driven supply chain planning with AI-assisted forecasting
  • Order management from customer order through shipment confirmation and invoicing
  • Spend analytics and category management for procurement optimization

🤝 Supplier Management

Oracle Procurement Cloud includes a full supplier lifecycle management capability — onboarding, qualification, performance tracking, and risk assessment — all in a single platform.

📊 Supply Chain Analytics

Real-time supply chain analytics built into Oracle SCM Cloud — inventory levels, order backlogs, supplier performance, and demand forecasts available without separate BI tools.

Modules

SCM & Procurement Modules We Implement

🛒

Oracle Purchasing Cloud

Purchase requisitions, purchase orders, blanket agreements, and receiving. Approval hierarchies, sourcing rules, and three-way match for invoice validation against POs and receipts.

🏆

Oracle Sourcing Cloud

RFQ and RFP management, supplier bidding, negotiation workbench, and award processing. Reduces sourcing cycle time and drives competitive pricing through structured supplier negotiations.

🏭

Oracle Inventory Management

Multi-organization inventory setup, subinventory and locator management, cycle counting, physical inventory, and lot/serial number tracking. Real-time visibility across your entire inventory network.

📋

Oracle Order Management Cloud

Sales order processing, order orchestration, ATP (Available to Promise), drop shipment, back-to-back orders, and customer returns management. Integrates with AR for billing and SCM for fulfillment.

📈

Oracle Supply Chain Planning

Demand management, supply planning, sales and operations planning (S&OP), and replenishment planning — powered by AI-assisted forecasting and constraint-based optimization.

🤝

Supplier Management

Supplier registration, qualification, performance management, and risk monitoring. Supplier portal setup for self-service invoice submission and PO collaboration.
