The Challenge

Why SOX Compliance on Oracle ERP Requires Specialist Knowledge

Oracle Fusion Cloud and EBS are powerful platforms — but their complexity creates significant SOX risk if not configured correctly. Out-of-the-box roles often contain Segregation of Duties conflicts. Audit trails are not fully enabled by default. And configuration changes can occur without adequate controls or logging.

NIRA LLC brings deep Oracle-specific SOX expertise — understanding both the regulatory requirements and the technical Oracle landscape needed to meet them.

  • Oracle's out-of-the-box roles frequently have SoD conflicts requiring custom role redesign
  • Audit trail coverage gaps exist in Oracle Fusion Cloud that require deliberate policy configuration
  • ERP configuration changes must be change-controlled and logged for SOX Section 404 compliance
  • Sensitive access (journal entry approval, payment processing, user admin) requires special monitoring

⚠️ Common SOX Risk in Oracle

Many organizations discover SoD conflicts and control gaps only during their annual external audit — at significant remediation cost and audit finding risk. Proactive control design prevents this.

🎯 Who This Is For

Public companies and subsidiaries subject to SOX Section 302 and 404, as well as organizations preparing for IPO or acquisition due diligence requiring clean internal control documentation on their Oracle platform.

What We Do

Our SOX Service Areas

📋

Section 404 Internal Control Assessment

We assess your Oracle ERP controls against SOX Section 404 requirements — documenting key controls over financial reporting processes including journal entry, procure-to-pay, order-to-cash, payroll, and financial close.

🔐

Segregation of Duties (SoD) Analysis

Comprehensive SoD conflict analysis across your Oracle role assignments. We identify incompatible function combinations (e.g., create supplier + approve payment), quantify risk, and provide remediation recommendations including custom role redesign.

🛡️

Oracle Risk Management Cloud Setup

Implementation and configuration of Oracle Risk Management Cloud — including access certifications, SoD policy definition, transaction monitoring rules, and continuous control monitoring dashboards for your compliance team.

📊

Audit Trail Configuration

Oracle Fusion Cloud audit policies must be explicitly enabled per object. We configure comprehensive audit trail coverage across financially significant objects — General Ledger, AP, AR, Fixed Assets — and design audit evidence reports for your auditors.

🔎

Sensitive Access Monitoring

Design and implementation of continuous monitoring controls for sensitive functions — super user access, manual journal entries, payment processing, system administration, and configuration changes — with alerting workflows for your control owners.

📁

Auditor Readiness & Documentation

We prepare control narrative documentation, control testing evidence packages, and walkthrough materials in the format expected by Big 4 and mid-tier external auditors — reducing audit preparation time and minimizing findings.
